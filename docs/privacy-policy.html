Privacy Policy — TaskEasy

Last updated: August 22, 2026

TaskEasy ("the app") is an independent, personal-project app built and operated by a single developer (Vignesh Jagannadhan) for managing tasks, group expenses, and a personal journal. This policy explains what information the app handles and how.

This app is a personal project. It is not operated by a company, does not run ads, does not use third-party analytics or advertising SDKs, and does not sell or share your data with anyone.

Information Collected

To create an account and use the app's features, the following information is collected and stored:

  • Account information: email address and password (password is never stored in plain text)
  • Profile information: any display name or profile details you choose to add
  • App content you create: tasks, group/expense records, and journal entries

The app does not request access to your device's camera, contacts, location, photos, or microphone, and does not collect any data beyond what you explicitly enter into the app.

How Information Is Used and Stored

  • Your login session token is stored securely on your device using the operating system's encrypted secure storage.
  • Tasks, expenses, and journal entries are sent over an encrypted (HTTPS) connection to a private backend server operated solely by the developer, and stored there so your data syncs across your own devices.
  • No data is used for advertising, profiling, or sold/shared with third parties.

Third-Party Services

The app uses the following third-party service for functional purposes only:

  • Google Fonts: the app may fetch font files from Google's font servers at runtime to render text. This is a standard font-delivery request and is not used for tracking or advertising.

No third-party analytics, crash-reporting, or advertising SDKs are integrated into the app.

Data Retention & Deletion

Your data is retained for as long as your account exists. You may request deletion of your account and all associated data at any time by contacting the developer at the email address below.

Children's Privacy

This app is not directed at children and is not intended for use by anyone under 13.

Changes to This Policy

This policy may be updated occasionally to reflect changes in the app. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last updated" date.
